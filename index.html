Welcome to MLWEC

Malineni Lakshmaiah Women's Engineering College is dedicated to providing quality technical education and creating opportunities for young women to develop into confident professionals and future leaders.

Our learning environment combines academic excellence, practical skills, innovation, technology and personality development.

  • Experienced faculty and mentors
  • Industry-oriented technical education
  • Modern laboratories and learning facilities
  • Career guidance and placement support
  • Women-focused leadership opportunities
